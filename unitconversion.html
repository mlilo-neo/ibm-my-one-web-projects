Unit Conversion
Temperature



To convert celsius to fanhrenheit yourself, use this formula replacing the 'C' with your temperature in celsius: (C * 9/5) + 32
Weight



To convert kilo to pounds yourself, use this formula replacing the 'kg' with your weight in kilo: (kg * 2.205)
Distance



To convert kilometers to miles yourself, use this formula replacing the `km` with your distance in kilometers: km ÷ 1.609
